About This Pattern

4-7-8 breathing is a relaxation technique that promotes calmness and reduces stress. Inhale for 4 seconds, hold for 7 seconds, and exhale for 8 seconds.

Note: This tool is for relaxation and wellness purposes only. If you experience dizziness or discomfort, stop immediately. Consult a healthcare provider for medical advice.
